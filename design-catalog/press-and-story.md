# Press, Awards and Story

**Compiled:** 2026-09-20.

Every quote below is attributed to the outlet that published it. Where a piece is paywalled and the full text could not be retrieved, that is stated rather than paraphrased into a fake quote.

## Why this document decides the brief

In the twelve months since reopening, Roses has won the **Detroit Free Press Restaurant of the Year**, landed on the **New York Times' 50 favourite restaurants in America**, taken a **three-star Detroit News review**, and made **Hour Detroit's Best New Restaurants**. Its own website mentions none of it, links to no reservation, and publishes the wrong hours.

The gap between the restaurant's reputation and its website is the entire business case. Nothing else in this research package matters as much.

## The Rose's Fine Food lineage

The honest version, which the site currently gets wrong in both directions.

- The **building** is a 1964 cinderblock diner on the east side.
- The **business** opened in **2014** as Rose's Fine Food and built its name on brunch. It ran nine years and closed in **2023**.
- Mitchell stepped away from daily service entirely. Between the closure and the reopening she led a local culinary arts program.
- It reopened in **2025** as **Roses**: dinner only, 28-29 seats, Polish American, produce-led.
- Mitchell calls the current restaurant **"my third business in this space."**

The current `/our-story` page claims *"A Detroit East Side Institution Since 1964"* and *"From our humble beginnings in 1964, Rose's has been serving comfort food with a Polish twist to generations of Detroit families."* **This is false.** It transfers the building's age onto the business and invents a multi-generation family history. It has to come down regardless of whether a rebuild happens.

**The correct framing, and the better story:** not an institution since 1964, but a beloved diner that closed, a chef who burned out and left, and a third attempt in the same room that won Restaurant of the Year inside twelve months. The nostalgia is real but it is nine years deep, not sixty-two, and the comeback is the part people actually care about. Every award write-up leads with the comeback.

## Room and design details, from press

Useful because the site has no photographs and these are the details the brand system has to match.

- Pressed-tin ceiling
- Mismatched vintage furniture; mismatched vintage mirrors
- Old woodwork
- Candles melted into sculptural wax formations, "wax-drenched"
- An open counter with backed counter stools; cozy banquettes
- Untamed floral arrangements, cut from a garden in the parking lot out back
- 28-29 seats inside
- **An adjoining garden space that holds roughly 120 more guests** — never mentioned on the website, and the obvious basis for a private-events page

## Facts to reuse verbatim

Verified across at least two independent sources.

- **Address:** 10551 E. Jefferson Ave., Detroit, MI 48214
- **Phone:** (313) 332-0404
- **Hours:** 4-10pm Sun, Mon, Wed, Thu · 4-11pm Fri, Sat · **closed Tuesday**
- **Reservations:** Resy, `resy.com/cities/detroit-mi/venues/roses`, up to 30 days out, dining room and bar. Walk-ins welcome
